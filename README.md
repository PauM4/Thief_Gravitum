During a family dinner in a grand old mansion, a priceless heirloom vanishes: The Brooch of Dawn, an ancient royal symbol set with a ruby, kept safely in a glass case inside the library. The theft is discovered minutes later, and all signs point to someone within the house.

The player takes on the role of Solei, an outsider (detective, distant relative, or close friend—depending on interpretation), who arrives at the mansion to uncover what truly happened that night. By speaking with each member of the household, examining objects, and collecting hidden clues, Solei must piece together the truth—and choose what to do with it.

The game unfolds in three main phases: an initial investigation through dialogue and observation, a deeper exploration of the mansion to find physical evidence, and a final moment of truth where the player decides the fate of the characters based on what they’ve learned.

The real thief is Nico, the son, who used the distraction of a heated family argument during dinner to sneak downstairs and steal the brooch. But his motive isn’t greed—days earlier, he discovered secret letters in the library linking his father, Lázaro, to a betrayal of the crown. The brooch was the key to accessing a hidden safe behind the shelves. Fearing his father might destroy the evidence, Nico hid the brooch in a hollow book in his room to protect his mother, Doña Mirena.

Each character has a layered role in the mystery:

Nico, seemingly detached and sarcastic, is in fact emotionally affected by the household tensions. Though impulsive, he acts out of loyalty and fear.

Doña Mirena, elegant and reserved, carries the weight of long-standing suspicions about her husband, but has never dared confront them.

Lázaro, the father, is a domineering and secretive man, avoiding any talk of politics or the brooch—a silence that speaks volumes.

Clara, the loyal housemaid, is discreet but observant. She notices the vitrine open after dinner and may know more than she lets on.

Solei, the protagonist, must navigate through these conflicting stories and decide: hide the truth, expose it, or walk away.

The player’s final choice leads to one of three endings:

Steal and flee – Solei takes the brooch and leaves in silence.

Steal and reveal – Solei exposes Nico, leaving the family in shock.

Leave it and tell the truth – Solei chooses honesty and lets justice unfold.

The Brooch of Dawn is a story of buried secrets, moral choices, and the fine line between protecting someone and doing what's right. Every clue matters, every word hides a possibility, and every decision changes the fate of a family on the edge.
